### <mark style="background: #04FF00A6;">Module Overview</mark>

Emphasises a human-centred approach to visualisation design, focusing on audience perception, interpretation, and learning.  

Incorporates principles of cognitive science, learning theories, and epistemology to guide the design and implementation of effective visualisations.  

Includes practical sessions to provide hands-on experience with data visualisation tools and techniques.  

Prepares students to apply their knowledge and skills to create impactful visualisations for real-world scenarios.

### <mark style="background: #04FF00A6;">Module Aim</mark>

To equip students with the foundational knowledge, principles, and practical skills required to transform raw data into meaningful and impactful visualisations.  

To foster the skills necessary to make sense of complex data and create visual solutions that transform information into actionable knowledge.  

To develop an understanding of how theories from psychology, statistics, data mining, graphic design, and information visualisation contribute to creating effective visual interfaces

### <mark style="background: #04FF00A6;">Continuous Assessment</mark>

<mark style="background: #04FF00A6;">Consists of FIVE (5) assessments, each worth 4% and are designed to:</mark> 
- Evaluate data visualisations using evaluation criteria covered during the modules  
- Assess ability to create essential data visualisation types and techniques  

<mark style="background: #04FF00A6;">Each task will be possible to complete during a scheduled lab session:</mark> 
- Demonstrations may be completed at any time before the deadline  
- You must demonstrate your completed work by a designated date  

<mark style="background: #04FF00A6;">Deadlines:</mark>
- Scheduled to avoid peak project workload  
- Released once assessment calendar has been released

<mark style="background: #04FF00A6;">Independent Project (30%)</mark>
